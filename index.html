SpendVault

SpendVault is a private personal finance, IRCTC train ticket, and online payment receipt tracker built with client-side AES-256-GCM encryption. Track monthly budgets, UPI and cash spending, recurring bills, and expenses across IRCTC, EatSure, Zomato, Swiggy, Amazon, and payment apps directly in your browser without requiring a login.

Optional Google Sign-In enables cross-browser encrypted cloud vault sync, and optional read-only Gmail receipt scanning parses merchant and IRCTC receipts locally inside your browser.
