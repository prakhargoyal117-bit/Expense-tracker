2. Description of Service

SpendVault is a personal finance utility for tracking monthly expenses, IRCTC train tickets, food and shopping app orders, recurring bills, and payment receipts using client-side AES-256-GCM encryption. SpendVault is intended for personal budgeting and record-keeping only and does not provide financial, tax, or accounting advice.

3. Encryption Passphrase & Backups

Because SpendVault encrypts your vault locally in your browser before saving or syncing to the cloud, we do not have access to your custom vault passphrase. If you set a custom passphrase and forget it, your encrypted vault cannot be recovered by SpendVault. We recommend storing your passphrase securely and periodically downloading an encrypted JSON backup.
