SpendVault Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 · Application: SpendVault (https://project2-beryl-eta.vercel.app)

Summary: SpendVault is a personal finance, IRCTC train ticket, and payment receipt tracker designed with local-first AES-256-GCM encryption. You can use SpendVault without creating an account or signing in. Google Sign-In and Gmail receipt scanning are strictly optional and user-initiated. We never sell, rent, or share your financial or Google account data with third parties.

1. Purpose of SpendVault

SpendVault helps individuals organize their personal monthly expenditures across online payment apps (UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED), IRCTC train bookings, food and shopping platforms (EatSure, Zomato, Swiggy, Amazon, Flipkart, Blinkit), recurring monthly bills, and manual cash entries. All ledger analytics, daily spend breakdowns, and receipt parsing execute locally in your web browser.

2. Information We Collect & How It Is Used

  • Local Expense & Budget Data (No Login Required): Transactions, budgets, recurring bills, and profile preferences you enter are encrypted locally in your browser using Web Crypto AES-256-GCM with a PBKDF2-SHA256 derived key and stored in your browser's local storage.
  • Optional Google Sign-In (Basic Profile): If you choose to click Sign in with Google to synchronize your encrypted vault across browsers or devices, we authenticate you through Firebase Authentication using basic Google profile scopes (openid, email, profile). We receive your name, email address, unique user ID, and profile photo solely to identify your account, personalize your profile display, and associate your encrypted vault record in Cloud Firestore.
  • Encrypted Cloud Vault Storage: When signed in, SpendVault uploads only your client-side AES-256-GCM encrypted ciphertext envelope (encrypted JSON payload, IV, and salt) to Google Cloud Firestore under your authenticated user ID so you can decrypt and access your vault on other browsers or mobile devices. Your vault passphrase is never transmitted to or stored on any server.
  • Optional Read-Only Gmail Receipt Scanning: Only if you explicitly click Connect Gmail Auto-Scan inside Apps & Sync, SpendVault requests read-only access to Gmail (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly). Access tokens are held strictly in browser memory for the active session and are never persisted to disk or sent to a backend server. SpendVault queries only matching order, IRCTC ticket, and payment receipt messages, extracts the merchant, amount, date, payment mode, and PNR/train metadata locally inside your browser, ignores OTPs, promotional messages, and incoming credits, and stores the extracted expense inside your encrypted vault.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use Disclosure)

SpendVault's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • SpendVault uses Google user data solely to provide and improve user-facing personal expense tracking and encrypted cross-browser synchronization features requested by the user.
  • SpendVault never sells Google user data, never uses Google user data for targeted advertising, credit-worthiness checks, or profiling, and never transfers Google user data to third-party data brokers.
  • SpendVault does not use any data obtained through Google Workspace or Gmail APIs to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.
  • Humans never read your Gmail messages or decrypted financial data; all parsing runs automatically inside your own browser.

4. Data Security & Encryption

All personal ledger entries, IRCTC ticket records, and receipt details are encrypted in your browser using 256-bit AES-GCM authenticated encryption before being written to local storage or synchronized to Firebase Firestore. Firestore security rules restrict read, write, and delete access strictly to the authenticated owner's verified user ID.

5. Data Retention, Revocation & Deletion

  • Delete All Data Immediately: You can permanently erase both your local encrypted vault and your synchronized cloud vault at any time inside SpendVault via Apps & Sync → End-to-End Encryption Vault → Delete All Data & Start Fresh, or from the Lock Screen.
  • Revoke Google Access: You can sign out inside SpendVault at any time and revoke SpendVault's permissions from your Google Account at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Data Deletion Requests: You may also request complete deletion of any cloud-stored vault records by emailing prakhargoyal117@gmail.com from your registered Google account email.

6. Third-Party Infrastructure

SpendVault uses Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore exclusively to authenticate optional user accounts and store client-side encrypted vault blobs, and Vercel to host the static web application.
